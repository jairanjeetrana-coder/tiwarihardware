Our Profile

Tiwari Hardware & Enterprises has been a fixture in Sandha Chhapra since 2001, supplying tools, plumbing fittings, paints and fasteners to homeowners, contractors and builders across Saran district. What began as a small counter shop has grown into a dependable one-stop supplier that local tradespeople rely on for every job, big or small.

Our Mission

We aim to make sure no one leaves without the right part — matched correctly the first time, priced fairly, and explained clearly. Our staff know the difference between a fitting that looks right and one that actually works.

Our Vision

To remain Chhapra's most dependable hardware supplier — the shop contractors call first and homeowners trust for honest advice, not just a sale.
